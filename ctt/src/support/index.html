Try it first

Choose Try document editing or CSV / Excel example on the home screen. The built-in examples let you compare, correct and download a result without using your own files.

Supported files

Compare TXT and DOCX text, CSV/TSV and XLSX tables, and PDFs with selectable text. Edit & export opens a single DOCX, PDF, CSV/TSV or XLSX. Files can be up to 2 MB.

PDF editing depends on the layout. Scans without text, forms and comments are not supported. Images are retained in supported workflows but are not compared. Word layout may differ from Microsoft Word. Spreadsheet comparison covers the selected sheet and checked columns; charts and images are not shown in the grid.

Your saved work

Wait for Saved in this browser before closing. Use Continue editing or the comparison resume control to reopen saved work. Website and extension storage are separate. Download a copy of the result when you finish.
