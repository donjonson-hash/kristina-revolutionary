01

Chrome & Edge

Install in developer mode and keep it ready to use.

Download for Chrome / Edge
  1. Download the ZIP and extract it into a folder.
  2. Open chrome://extensions or edge://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode and select Load unpacked.
  4. Select the extracted folder. Pin Compare These Texts to your browser toolbar.

To update, replace the files in the same folder and reload the extension. Keep that folder on your computer.

02

Firefox

Try a temporary installation for the current browser session.

Download for Firefox
  1. Download and extract the ZIP.
  2. Open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox.
  3. Select Load Temporary Add-on and choose manifest.json in the extracted folder.
  4. Click the Compare These Texts icon to compare documents.

Requires Firefox 142 or later. Load the temporary add-on again after restarting Firefox.

Review each difference. Keep moving.

Use A or keep B, move straight to the next difference, and download your reviewed document. Edit Word, CSV and Excel tables and save your progress as you go. Switch between Document and Text, preview your edits with images, and download your PDF. Longer replacements wrap, move the content below and add pages while retaining unchanged page artwork in supported layouts. Complex overlapping layouts and section reordering may require the separate text-only PDF option.

On the website or in your browser toolbar

Both versions compare files on your device. The extension works offline. Saved work is stored separately on the website and in the extension, without automatic syncing.
